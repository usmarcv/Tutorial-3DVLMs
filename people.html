Co-authors

Afonso Paiva

ICMC-USP

Associate Professor at the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Sciences of the University of São Paulo (ICMC-USP). He holds a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from PUC-Rio (2007) and an M.Sc. in Mathematics from IMPA (2003). His research covers computer graphics, visualization, image processing, computational physics, and data science.

Jeová Farias

Bowdoin College

Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Bowdoin College. He is broadly interested in unsupervised problems in computer vision and machine learning, using discrete optimization, statistics, and deep learning for image segmentation and clustering.

Tiago Novello

IMPA

Assistant Professor at IMPA, member of the Visgraf Lab, and visiting assistant professor at Stanford, working with Leonidas Guibas. His research spans computer vision, computer graphics, and machine learning, with a focus on implicit neural representations, neural rendering, and geometry processing.

Moacir Ponti

ICMC-USP

Associate Professor of Computer Science at ICMC-USP and Machine Learning Expert at Mercado Livre. His research focuses on deep and representation learning across multiple data modalities, including image, audio, speech, and tabular data.
