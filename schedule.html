Tutorial: An overview of 3D Vision-Language Models

Program

The tutorial was presented at SIBGRAPI 2026 by Márcus Lobo and Vitor Matias, in two parts, with a hands-on session closing Part 1.

  • Date and time: Tuesday, September 29, 2026, 14:00–17:30
  • Room: D
  • Location: Federal University of Goiás (UFG), Goiânia, GO, Brazil
  • Official listing: tutorial #266 in the SIBGRAPI 2026 tutorials program
Topic Description
Part 1 — Foundations and contrastive alignment   Slides
Goals and motivation Why 3D and language: applications in robotics, LiDAR, medical imaging and engineering; the move beyond closed-set 3D recognition; industry and research impact.
3D representations and datasets Point clouds, meshes, multi-view images and 3D Gaussian splatting; where the language comes from; ShapeNet, Objaverse / Objaverse-XL and Objaverse-LVIS.
3D VLMs vs. 3D VLLMs The alignment paradigm (similarity scores and rankings) against the generative paradigm (encoder, projector and LLM producing text); overview of the 3D VLM training pipeline.
Input and encoders (3D, image, text) triplets; image, text and 3D encoders with projection layers; 3D encoder options and a closer look at Point-BERT and masked point modeling.
Geometric view of contrastive alignment Frozen OpenCLIP image and text embeddings as the semantic reference space; L2-normalization, the unit hypersphere and cosine similarity before and after alignment.
Contrastive alignment for 3D VLMs Alignment as batch matching, the similarity matrix, the symmetric InfoNCE loss and its PyTorch implementation; key ingredients: data scale, augmentation, batch size, hard negatives, temperature and compute.
A taxonomy of 3D VLMs Five families, from fixed labels to open vocabulary to language generation.
Hands-on Contrastive learning for 3D VLMs, end to end, in a notebook: point cloud → 3D encoder → joint embedding space → similarity matrix → InfoNCE loss → training loop.
Part 2 — Model families, tasks and applications   Slides
Recap Problem statement, contrastive alignment, and 3D VLMs vs. 3D VLLMs.
Closed-set recognition and projection-based adaptation Fixed classifiers (MVCNN, PointNet, DGCNN, Point-BERT) and their limits; rendering 3D into 2D views for frozen CLIP, with PointCLIP V2.
Native joint embeddings Training a dedicated 3D encoder into a shared space with image and text: OpenShape, Uni3D and 3D-MRL.
Language-aligned 3D Gaussian splatting Gaussians as the 3D modality: UniGS and related methods.
3D Vision-Large Language Models The encoder → projector → LLM recipe and two-stage training: PointLLM, ShapeLLM, PointAlign and N3D-VLM.
From model families to tasks Zero-shot classification, cross-modal retrieval and spatial reasoning.
Embodied AI Vision-language-action models and the role of 3D data: PointVLA and GR00T N1.
